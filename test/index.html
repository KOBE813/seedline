Run the three tests.

Check a Trump Account contribution program against the proposed § 1.128-3 rules: contributions, eligibility, and the 55% average benefits test. You get a pass or fail on each, with the fix if one fails.

Step 01 — Settings

Plan year and thresholds

$160,000 is the HCE threshold for 2026 determinations (Notice 2025-67), compared to the employee's pay in the look-back year (2025). Employees who own more than 5% are always HCEs. The optional top-paid-group election isn't modeled.

The three tests · § 1.128-3

  • Contributions & benefits: can't favor HCEs or their dependents.
  • Eligibility: the classification can't favor HCEs. Safe harbor: ratio percentage ≥ 90% minus ¾ point per point of NHCE concentration over 60%.
  • Average benefits: NHCE average ≥ 55% of HCE average.

Left out of tests 2 and 3: employees who haven't reached 21 and 1 year of service, and union employees not in the plan. Pilot-match dollars are ignored for tests 1 and 3.

Test 1 is facts-and-circumstances. This tool flags HCEs getting more than NHCEs under the plan, but can't prove a pass.
