Draft your Section 128 written plan.

Answer six short steps. You get a Trump Account contribution plan and a one-page employee notice, ready for your advisor to review. Nothing you type leaves this browser.

    Built on

    Proposed regs REG-101355-26 (Aug 11, 2026) and Notice 2025-68. Proposed rules can change. See sources

    Step 01 — Employer

    Who is adopting the plan?

    Owners in this entity type (partners, sole proprietors, or more-than-2% S corporation shareholders) can sponsor the plan but can't receive contributions under it. The plan text excludes them automatically.

    Step 02 — Plan year

    When does it start?

    A calendar plan year lines up with the $2,500 annual exclusion, which is counted per calendar year, and with W-2 reporting.

    Step 03 — Eligibility

    Which employees can take part?

    Optional exclusions

    Heads up: part-time isn't one of the automatic testing exclusions. It's allowed only if the eligibility classification still passes the nondiscrimination test. Ask your advisor to run it.

    Under the proposed rules, employees under 21 with less than one year of service, and certain union employees, can be left out when you test for discrimination. A waiting period of 12 months or less fits inside that.

    Step 04 — Contributions

    How much will you contribute?

    The $2,500 exclusion is per employee, not per child, and counts both your contributions and any salary reductions. Salary reduction can go only to a dependent's account, and needs a separate Section 125 cafeteria plan.

    Step 05 — Payroll

    Who runs payroll?

    Your payroll setup needs to treat contributions as excluded from federal income tax but still subject to FICA and FUTA, and report them in Form W-2 box 12 with code TA.

    Sources

    What this is built on.

    Every rule above comes from these documents. Where the rules are proposed or unclear, the draft says so.

    1. Proposed regulations REG-101355-26, 91 Fed. Reg. 51611 (Aug. 11, 2026), FR Doc. 2026-16314: proposed §§ 1.128-1 to 1.128-3. Written plan contents, certification, notice, Jan 31 statement, salary reduction, nondiscrimination, excluded employees, correction.
    2. Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-37, same proposed regulations as published by the IRS.
    3. IRC § 128 (added by Pub. L. 119-21, 2025), as summarized in the preamble: $2,500 exclusion per employee, indexed after 2027; plan must meet rules similar to § 129(d)(2), (3), (6), (7) and (8).
    4. IRS Notice 2025-68, Q&A I-1 and I-2, as quoted in the preamble: limit is per employee, not per dependent.
    5. 2026 General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3, as cited in the preamble: box 12, code TA.
    6. Troutman Pepper Locke and EY Tax News summaries of the proposed regulations.
