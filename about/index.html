Built for the paperwork around the deduction.

Who’s behind Seedline and how to reach us.

Hi, I’m Dhruv.

I’m the founder of Seedline. When Trump Accounts became law, I saw that payroll apps could handle the deduction, but employers still needed everything around it: a written plan, employee notices, sign-up forms, nondiscrimination testing, and year-end reporting. Seedline builds those pieces, so employers and the accountants who advise them can set up a Section 128 program without starting from a blank page.

Seedline is free during beta. Every rule it uses is sourced and marked where the rules are still proposed.

How to reach us

Seedline is online only, with no phone line, calls, or demos. Email dhruva@mail.grokbot.com and we’ll reply by email.
