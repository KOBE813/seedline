Employee sign-up and certification.

A printable form employees fill out to name the Trump Account that gets your contribution, with the certification the proposed rules call for. Hand it out with the plan notice.

Step 02 — Form options

What should it include?

The form collects the three statements the proposed rules list in § 1.128-2(d)(4): the beneficiary is you or your dependent, their date of birth, and that you know of no reason they can't get a contribution. You can rely on the form unless you know it's wrong, but you still need your own reasonable check that each account is a real Trump Account.

Prints on one or two pages

What the form covers

  • Account designation (beneficiary, trustee, account number)
  • Certification under § 1.128-2(d)(4)
  • Optional pre-tax election, dependents only
  • Employer-only verification box

Per a law-firm summary of the Sept. 2026 temporary regulations, an unclaimed auto-created account can't receive employer contributions. The form asks employees to confirm the account is claimed.
