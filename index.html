05 — FAQ

Plain answers.

Is Seedline available now?

Not yet. Seedline is in early development. Join early access and we'll email you when it's ready.

Are the rules final?

No. The IRS rules for employer contributions are proposed regulations (Aug 2026) and may change. We'll update Seedline as the rules change.

Is the contribution tax-free?

Under the proposed rules, employer contributions up to $2,500 per employee per year are free of federal income tax for the employee. They're still subject to FICA and FUTA. Ask your tax advisor about your situation.

Does it replace my payroll app?

No. Seedline is designed to work alongside any payroll app, which handles the deduction and W-2 reporting.

Will I need to get on a call?

No. Everything is online and by email. No calls, demos, or meetings.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. Seedline provides general educational information and document tools. Have a qualified tax advisor or benefits counsel review your program.
