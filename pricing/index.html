Pricing FAQ

Before you buy.

Is it really free right now?

Yes. Every tool is free during the beta and nothing is charged. If paid plans launch, we'll email early-access members first.

Is my data stored by Seedline?

No. Your answers and client data stay in your browser's local storage. Export a JSON backup to move between devices.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. Seedline produces templates and checklists based on proposed IRS regulations. Have a qualified tax advisor or benefits attorney review everything before you use it.

What happens when paid plans launch?

We'll email you first. The planned terms include a 14-day refund (see the draft refund policy) and cancel-anytime for the Accountant plan.

Are you affiliated with the IRS?

No. Seedline is not affiliated with the IRS, the U.S. Treasury, any payroll provider, or any government agency.
