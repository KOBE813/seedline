Payroll setup for Trump Account contributions.

Which payroll providers support Trump Account (Section 128) employer contributions as of October 2026, how to set them up, and the tax treatment every provider must follow.

Guide · Checked Oct 12, 2026 · 5 min read

Proposed rules. Much of this is based on proposed regulations REG-101355-26 (Aug. 11, 2026). They aren’t final and may change, though employers may rely on them for plan years before final rules. Not legal or tax advice.

What every payroll setup needs

  • Excluded from federal income tax up to $2,500 per employee per year
  • Still subject to FICA and FUTA (proposed regulations)
  • Reported in W-2 box 12, code TA
  • Tracked per employee across all children, and per account against the $5,000 limit

QuickBooks

Create a Company Contribution item with tax tracking type “Trump Accounts”, enter the annual maximum on each employee’s profile, and run any extra employee money as an after-tax deduction.

IRIS Payroll Relief

Add “TRUMP Account – Employer (TA)” under Employer Setup → Deductions and assign one row per child. IRIS’s release notes mention W-2 code “TT”; the IRS code is TA, so confirm with IRIS. ⚑

Gusto

Fund contributions outside Gusto and confirm W-2 reporting before year end. Gusto’s article says FICA/FUTA guidance hadn’t been issued; the August 2026 proposed rules say FICA and FUTA apply. ⚑

ADP

Ask your ADP rep how to set up a section 128 contribution code, and confirm the tax treatment above.

FAQ

Does Gusto support Trump Account contributions?

Not for setup or funding, according to Gusto’s help center as of October 2026. Gusto says it is working on W-2 reporting for contributions made outside Gusto.

Does QuickBooks Payroll support Trump Accounts?

Yes. QuickBooks has a Company Contribution item with the tax tracking type “Trump Accounts” that reports in W-2 box 12, code TA.

What W-2 code is used for Trump Account contributions?

Box 12, code TA, per the 2026 General Instructions for Forms W-2 and W-3.
